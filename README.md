Poultry farming plays a crucial role in the global agricultural economy, yet small-scale 

farmers face significant challenges such as high disease mortality, market inefficiencies, and 

limited access to veterinary services. PoultryConnect is an innovative, technology-driven 

solution designed to empower farmers by integrating IoT-based environmental monitoring, 

AI-driven disease detection, and a digital marketplace. The system utilizes temperature, 

humidity, and ammonia sensors to collect real-time data, which is analyzed using AI 

algorithms to predict disease outbreaks and alert farmers via a mobile app and SMS 

notifications. By providing direct market access, PoultryConnect helps farmers bypass 

middlemen, ensuring better profits and economic stability. This solution is designed to be 

affordable, scalable, and regionally adaptive, addressing the unique needs of small-scale 

farmers in rural areas. With the potential to reduce disease-related losses by 50% and 

increase productivity by 15%, PoultryConnect contributes to sustainable agriculture, 

economic growth, and improved rural livelihoods.
